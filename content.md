## PROGRAMS

### 1. Jolly Phonics
**What it is:** A systematic synthetic phonics approach that teaches the sounds of English so children can read and spell — understanding sound–letter relationships instead of memorising words.
**Learning path:** Hear → Recognise → Blend → Read → Spell → Write
**Covers:** 42 letter sounds, letter-sound recognition, sound identification, oral blending, blending, segmenting, reading simple words, word building, tricky words, digraphs, alternative spellings, spelling patterns, reading fluency, writing, dictation, vocabulary, pronunciation, reading comprehension.
**Rules & patterns:** ck, floss rule, magic e / split digraph, double letters, digraphs, diphthongs, alternative spellings, and other age-appropriate patterns.
**Who can join:**
- Children — build reading and spelling foundations
- School students — strengthen phonics, reading, spelling, English
- Parents — understand phonics and support children at home
- Teachers — learn systematic methodology and classroom strategies
- Educators — professional skills for early literacy
- Adults & homemakers — improve reading, pronunciation, spelling, communication

From phonics to structured grammar. Topics: parts of speech (nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs), tenses, sentence formation, punctuation, vocabulary, spelling, grammar rules, reading comprehension, writing. Goal: use English accurately and confidently.

## FOR PARENTS — WHY PHONICS MATTERS
**Common question:** "My child knows the alphabet. Why is reading still difficult?"
**Answer:** Knowing letter names is different from knowing the sounds letters make. Phonics teaches how sounds combine into words — c – a – t → cat — so children can decode new words instead of relying on memorisation.
**How parents can help:** listen to children read, practise sounds regularly, encourage rather than pressure, read stories together, build vocabulary, celebrate small improvements, create a positive reading environment.
